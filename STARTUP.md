# Steanews: startup plan

## The honest starting point
Right now Steanews is a nicely designed reader for other sites' RSS feeds. That is not a business yet:
- Anyone can copy it, and Google News and Feedly already do it.
- Many publishers' feed terms allow personal or non-commercial use only. Putting ads next to their headlines and images can break those terms. Check each source before you earn money from it.

So the plan is to add something that is yours.

## Pick one angle (don't do all of them)
1. **Curation with opinion** (recommended start). A daily "5 things that matter" in your own words, linking to the sources. Opinion and summary are yours, so there is no copyright problem. Costs nothing to start.
2. **A niche audience.** One game or genre only (for example Steam deals and PC releases, or Souls-likes). Small audiences are loyal and easier to rank for.
3. **A tool, not just news.** A Steam sale and release tracker, a wishlist alert, a "what to play next" page. Tools earn more than headlines and get returning visitors.
4. **French-language gaming news.** jeuxvideo.com dominates, but there is room for a cleaner, faster site for French and Arabic speakers if that is your audience.

## How it earns money (in order of effort)
| Stage | Method | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Steam and store **affiliate links** (Humble, Fanatical, Green Man Gaming, Amazon) on deal and release pages | Some traffic, no inventory |
| 2 | **Display ads** (Google AdSense, then Ezoic or Mediavine) | Original content and about 10k visits a month |
| 3 | **Newsletter** with a sponsor slot | 1,000+ subscribers |
| 4 | **Sponsored posts** from indie studios, clearly labelled | A real audience |
| 5 | **Pro tier**: ad-free, alerts, saved lists | Accounts and a backend |

Realistic expectation: the first months earn close to nothing. Income comes from consistent traffic, usually after 6 to 12 months.

## 90-day plan
- **Weeks 1-2:** Buy a domain, put the site online (Cloudflare Pages or Netlify, free). Add an About page, Privacy page and Contact page. These are needed for ad networks.
- **Weeks 3-6:** Pick your angle. Publish your own short posts 3 times a week. Add a newsletter signup (Buttondown or Substack free tier).
- **Weeks 7-10:** Add affiliate links. Post daily on Reddit, X and TikTok where gamers already are.
- **Weeks 11-13:** Apply for AdSense. Check what people clicked, then double down on that.

## Tech to-do (when you are ready)
- Small backend that fetches the feeds on a schedule, so you stop depending on rss2json's free limit.
- A database for stories, so posts have stable URLs. Good for search engines.
- Your own post editor, so you can write articles in the same design.
- Accounts and saved stories on the server (today they live only in the browser).

## Legal checklist
- Show only headline, a short excerpt and a link back. Never copy full articles or reuse images without permission. Switch to your own images or licensed ones when you monetize.
- Label sponsored posts and affiliate links.
- Add a privacy policy and cookie notice (needed for ads, especially for EU visitors).
- "Steam" is Valve's trademark. Steanews is close enough to cause trouble, so consider a more distinct name before you invest in branding. You can keep the Steam-inspired colors; those are fine.

## First thing to do this week
Decide your angle and audience, then write 10 original posts for it. Everything else builds on that.
