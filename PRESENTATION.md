# Podium: presentation notes

## One-line pitch
Podium is one calm place for Formula 1 and tennis fans: live countdowns, standings, and the best headlines from the top publishers, personalised to the drivers, teams and players you follow.

## The problem
Fans of both sports jump between many sites: one for F1 news, another for tennis scores, a third for rankings and calendars. Each is busy, ad-heavy and built for one sport.

## How Podium compares
| Site | Strong at | Podium's edge |
|---|---|---|
| Formula1.com / F1 TV | Official data, premium live timing | Free, one feed for two sports, no paywall |
| Autosport, Motorsport.com, PlanetF1, The Race | Deep F1 reporting | Podium links to them and adds tennis, so readers don't need five tabs |
| Sofascore | Live scores, rankings, head-to-head, a personal feed | Calmer design, news and data in one view, two sports instead of dozens |
| TNNS / Tennishead, Tennis 24 | Tennis draws, favourites, filters | Same follow-and-filter idea, shared with F1 |
| ESPN SportsCenter | Favourite teams, an inbox of followed topics | Podium's "For you" tab does the same without an account |

What the market shows people want (so we built it): favourites and follow, topic and source filters, daily roundups, head-to-head comparisons, and fan predictions.

## Demo flow (about 3 minutes)
1. **Home:** "Every lap. Every set." The top story has a real photo. Two wait tiles count down to the next race and the next tennis event, live.
2. **Formula 1 tab:** the race weekend schedule in your local time, then *Add the race to your calendar*. Switch standings between Drivers and Constructors.
3. **Head to head:** compare Antonelli and Hamilton, then change a driver in the dropdown. The bar and the gap update instantly.
4. **Make your call:** pick a winner for the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
5. **Follow:** tap a star on a driver, then open **For you** to see only their stories. Tap a name to filter the news to them.
6. **Tennis tab:** rankings (switch ATP and WTA), the ATP Finals tile, head to head, and a prediction.
7. **Source filter:** filter the news to one publisher. Open a story and show *Read the full article* going to the original publisher.
8. **Phone:** shrink the window. Same product, one column.
9. **Partners section** at the bottom: the business model.

## Business model (plan, no invented numbers)
Affiliate links (tickets, streaming, merchandise), sponsorship, and a newsletter. Details in `STARTUP.md`.

## Be honest about limits (investors respect this)
- News is headline, excerpt and image, with a link to the publisher. Full articles stay on their sites.
- Standings and rankings come from free public data (Jolpica for F1, ESPN for tennis). A paid data feed would be needed at scale.
- Predictions and follows are saved on the user's device. Shared leaderboards and accounts would need a backend.
- The contact address in the partners section is a placeholder until you set up a real inbox.
- There are no traffic or revenue numbers yet. Don't claim any.
